🍋 Lemon Drizzle Cake

Ingredients

  • 225 g self-raising flour (1¾ cups)
  • 175 g caster sugar (¾ cup), plus 75 g (⅓ cup) for the drizzle
  • 175 g unsalted butter, at room temperature (¾ cup)
  • 3 medium eggs
  • 2 tablespoons full-fat milk
  • Zest and juice of 2 large lemons
  • 1 teaspoon baking powder

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 180°C (160°C fan, gas 4). Grease and line a 900 g (2 lb) loaf tin.
  2. Beat the butter and 175 g sugar together until pale and fluffy.
  3. Beat in the eggs one at a time, then fold in the flour, baking powder, lemon zest and milk.
  4. Spoon into the tin and bake for 45–50 minutes, until a skewer comes out clean.
  5. Mix the lemon juice with the 75 g sugar. Prick the warm cake all over and pour the drizzle on top. Leave to cool in the tin.
